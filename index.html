          How to play

          One wild animal a day, three rounds, 3,000 points to win.

          1. 1
            Name the animal. You get a close-up photo and five guesses. Each miss zooms the photo out, unlocks a clue, and tells you how closely your guess is related: same class, order, family or genus. Fewer guesses, more points. If you never get it, your closest relative still earns up to 350.
          2. 2
            Estimate the wild population. Anything inside the published range scores full marks. Outside it, the right order of magnitude is what counts.
          3. 3
            Paint where it lives. Colour in the map cells that make up its native range. Cells next to the true range count for half, so near misses still score.

          Missing round 1 does not end the game: the animal is revealed and you carry on. A new animal arrives at midnight, your time. Play every day to build a streak. Score 1,500 or more on a daily and the animal joins your zoo. From a 3-day streak you can retake one missed animal a day. A first retake needs 1,750, and every further retake of the same animal needs 250 more.

            Privacy

            Short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device and collects nothing about you.

            What is stored. Your streak, scores, zoo and unfinished game are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you copy a backup code yourself.

            What is not collected. There are no accounts, no advertising cookies, no tracking and no analytics.

            Other sites. Source and photo-credit links open Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and other sites, which have their own privacy policies.

            Deleting your data. Clear this site's data in your browser settings and everything is gone.

            This page will be updated before accounts, leaderboards, analytics or advertising are ever added.
